date: 2026-08-23
authors:
  - me
---

I reported a bug in Wallos, an open-source subscription manager. The two-factor authentication QR code did not appear when the username contained non-ASCII characters.

The report is [issue #1147](https://github.com/ellite/Wallos/issues/1147). The fix proposed there shipped in v5.4.5 on 23 August 2026 and is still present in v5.8.3.
