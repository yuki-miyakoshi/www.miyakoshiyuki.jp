summary: This study proposes a method to quantitatively evaluate the effect of assistive devices and the physical load on workers in electrical construction, using motion data from motion capture. Much of the work still depends on human labor, and overhead tasks such as installing crossarms place a heavy load on the body. Joint-angle distributions of workers with different years of experience are visualized with alpha shapes so that the load reduction from assistive devices can be shown both visually and quantitatively.
